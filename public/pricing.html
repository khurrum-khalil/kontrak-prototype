Questions we get
Do students ever pay a subscription?
No. There is no student subscription. Kontrak earns only from money already paid to you, and only from your second job onwards.
When is the fee taken?
When escrow is released to you. The fee is calculated at release: 0% if it is your first job, 5% after that, plus 3% if you boosted the bid. A month without earnings is a month without fee.
What does the boost do?
A boosted bid is shown first to the client. You pay nothing upfront. The extra 3% is taken on payout only if the bid wins and the work is released.
Are taxes and social insurance included?
No. The prototype shows gross amounts. Payroll deductions that apply to employment are not modelled yet.
