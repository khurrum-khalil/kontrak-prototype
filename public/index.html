The compliance engine

Three gates before any bid

  1. Eligible?
    Filtered before ranking. Ineligible work never reaches the feed.
  2. Within the cap?
    Hours must fit the 20 h week and the remaining days, or the bid is blocked.
  3. Above the floor?
    Fee divided by scoped hours must reach €13.90 an hour.
A hard gate, not a warning. Example: €480 for 14 scoped hours is €34.29 an hour, well above the €13.90 floor.

Questions

What students and clients ask first

Is this legal for my permit?
Kontrak engages you as an employee, not as a freelancer. A §16b student permit generally allows employment within 140 full or 280 half days a year. We record your permit wording at sign-up and filter every brief against it. Your Ausländerbehörde has the final word on your individual permit.
Do students ever pay to find work?
No. Finding work is free and your first job carries no fee. After that, 5% is taken from money already paid to you. No earnings in a month means no fee.
When does Kontrak earn?
Only when you are paid. We take 5% of the amount released to you from your second job onwards, plus 3% if you chose the optional boost. Clients post briefs for free.
What if I run out of days?
Bids that need more days or hours than you have left are blocked before you can send them. Hours that would go over a limit cannot be logged either. Your day account resets on 1 January.
What happens to the money if the client disappears?
The client pays the fee into escrow before you start, and it stays there until the client signs off. In this prototype, escrow and payouts are simulated. A live version would hold funds with a licensed payment provider.
What is a Day-Cap Safe brief?
A brief whose scoped hours fit a 20-hour week across its timeline, that pays at least the €13.90 statutory minimum per hour, and that sits under German law. Your own day account is checked again when you bid.
Who verifies clients?
Every client gives a Handelsregister number at sign-up. In this prototype the check is simulated: HRB and HRA numbers pass, anything else goes to manual review by the Kontrak team.
Does this replace advice from my Ausländerbehörde?
No. Kontrak tracks the limits and blocks work that would break them, but your Ausländerbehörde and the wording on your permit decide your case. Ask them if you are unsure.
