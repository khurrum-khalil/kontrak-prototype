Bring Kontrak to your campus

International offices answer the same question every semester: where can I work legally? Kontrak gives your students a checked answer.

What a partnership looks like
  1. Welcome weeks and emails

    A short session on legal work and the day account, plus one email from the international office.

  2. A free check for every student

    The Day-Cap Checker works without an account. Students see how many days they have left.

  3. Clients signed first

    We sign local firms and startups before launch, so students find briefs on day one.

  4. Student ambassadors

    Referrals, 0% fee on the first job and short question-and-answer videos.
