The day account · §16b(3) AufenthG

140 full days, 280 half days, one calendar year

A day of more than four hours is a full day. Four hours or fewer is a half day. One full day equals two half days, and the two can be mixed.

The account runs from 1 January to 31 December, not with your academic year. If you arrive in September, you still have the whole allowance for that year.

Freelance and self-employed work needs separate permission

A §16b permit covers employment, not self-employment. Freelancing generally requires approval from your Ausländerbehörde, and the wording on your permit governs. Kontrak engages you as an employee for this reason, and records your permit wording at sign-up. The decision sits with your authority, not with us.
