AGB · terms of use

Draft. Not legally reviewed.

These draft terms describe how the prototype works. They are not binding terms of service.

1. Prototype

Kontrak is a student prototype. Payments, escrow, signatures and verification are simulated. No money moves and no contract created here is legally binding.

2. Fees

Finding work is free. No fee applies to a student's first job. From the second job, 5% of the amount released to the student is deducted, plus 3% if the student chose the optional boost. Posting a brief is free for clients.

3. Compliance checks

Kontrak checks permit type, day account, weekly hours and the statutory minimum wage before a bid is allowed. These checks rely on the information users provide. They do not replace advice from the Ausländerbehörde.

4. No legal advice

Everything on this site is information, not legal advice.
