Datenschutz

Draft. Not legally reviewed.

This draft explains what the prototype stores. A full privacy notice under Art. 13 GDPR will be written with a qualified person before launch.

What we store

  • Account data you enter at sign-up, stored in Excel files on the machine running the prototype.
  • A session cookie (kt_session) after you log in. It is strictly necessary and expires after 7 days.
  • Your language choice and the cookie-notice choice, in your browser's local storage.
  • Day-Cap Checker runs without personal data: permit type, days used and weekly hours only.
  • Waitlist and partnership enquiries, with the email address you give us.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.
  • No sharing with third parties. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time. In the prototype, the team can delete rows directly in the Excel files.
