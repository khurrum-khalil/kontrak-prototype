How the compliance model works

One platform, country-specific compliance modules. Germany is the first and only live module.

Information, not legal advice. Kontrak is a student prototype. It is not approved by any authority. Your residence permit, your Ausländerbehörde and qualified advisers decide individual cases.

1. Employment, not freelancing

Non-EU students on a §16b residence permit may take up employment within limits. Self-employment and freelancing generally need separate permission from the Ausländerbehörde. Platforms that list workers as self-employed are therefore off limits for most of our students. On Kontrak, every engagement is an employment relationship for a single brief, documented in an engagement letter under German law.

2. Three gates before any bid

Eligible? The student must be verified, the brief open, the jurisdiction live, and the student's permit type allowed by the brief. Ineligible briefs never reach the matched feed.

Within the cap? The bid's hours, split into sessions, must fit the remaining day account (140 full or 280 half days per calendar year) and the 20-hour weekly cap, spread evenly from today to the deadline. Days already reserved for signed work count too. A bid that does not fit is blocked, not warned.

Above the floor? The fee divided by the hours must reach the statutory minimum wage in force during the work: €13.90 per hour in 2026 and €14.60 from 1 January 2027. If monthly earnings would pass the €603 Minijob ceiling, we flag it without blocking.

The browser shows the result instantly, but the server runs every gate again and its decision is final. Each decision is written to the audit log.

3. The day account

Every hour logged updates the day account immediately. A date with more than four hours counts as a full day; four hours or fewer as a half day. If a second entry on the same date takes it past four hours, the half day becomes a full day. Hours that would exceed the account or the weekly cap cannot be logged.

4. Contracts, escrow and traceability

Each engagement has one contract ID, for example KTK-2026-0412. The engagement letter, the escrow record, the messages and the hour log all hang off it. The client funds escrow after both sides sign. The student requests release on delivery; the client signs off, and the payment is released. Once released, a payment can't be reversed.

5. What is simulated

  • Escrow, card and SEPA payments, and payouts. No money moves.
  • E-signatures. Typing your name and ticking the consent box records a name and timestamp only.
  • Verification emails. The link is shown on screen.
  • The Handelsregister check. HRB and HRA numbers pass; others go to manual review.

6. What the team still has to validate

Legal wording of the engagement letter with a qualified person, permit practice per Ausländerbehörde, payroll and social-insurance handling, and real providers for payments, signatures and register checks.
