About Kontrak

It started with a founder's search for work

Finding part-time work is hard when you are adapting to a new country. German is a second wall. And a vacancy is not enough: is this job, or this contract, compatible with my residence status?

Our core message

Legality is the product. Matching is a feature.

A starting insight, not proof. We are validating it with students and employers.
