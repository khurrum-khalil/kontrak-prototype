How many days do I have left?

Enter what you have worked this calendar year. The checker counts your remaining full and half days, your weekly hours, and the briefs you could take today.

How the count works

Since 1 March 2024, a §16b student permit allows 140 full days or 280 half days of work per calendar year. A day of more than 4 hours is a full day. A day of 4 hours or fewer is a half day. Two half days equal one full day.

During the lecture period you may also work no more than 20 hours a week. Kontrak applies this weekly cap year-round in the prototype, as a conservative default.

The account resets on 1 January, not with the academic year.

We store each run without any personal data: only the permit type, days used and weekly hours, so we can see how students use the tool.
Information, not legal advice. Days worked for other employers count too. Your permit wording and your Ausländerbehörde decide your case.
