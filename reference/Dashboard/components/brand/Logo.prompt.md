One-sentence: the Kontrak logo — use `variant="lockup"` in headers and footers, `variant="mark"` for avatars, favicons and tight nav bars.

```jsx
<Logo size={44} />
<Logo variant="mark" size={28} />
<Logo tone="onNavy" size={56} />
```

Notes: never recolour the mark; the gold lower arm is the "student side" of the K and is fixed. The hairline divider only appears on `lockup` at size ≥ 56. On navy use `tone="onNavy"` (translucent tile, lighter gold arm); on a gold field use `tone="onGold"`.
